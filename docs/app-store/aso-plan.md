# ASO Plan

## Primary Positioning

Nuju should be positioned as a private AI mood journal, not as therapy, diagnosis, or a clinical mental health app.

Core promise:

> Feel understood quickly, then keep a private place to write, speak, and notice emotional patterns.

## Metadata Set

App name:

`Nuju: AI Mood Journal`

Subtitle:

`Private emotional reflection`

Promotional text:

`Feel understood in one minute. Nuju helps you turn crowded thoughts into private mood notes, voice journals, and gentle AI reflection.`

Keywords:

`tracker,diary,feelings,self care,wellness,voice,mental clarity,gratitude,habits,overthinking`

## Screenshot Story

1. Emotional reveal: the core conversion hook.
2. Guided check-in: shows the low-friction start.
3. Journal entry: proves daily utility.
4. Mood patterns: proves long-term subscription value.
5. AI companion: shows premium support while avoiding clinical claims.
6. Optional privacy/control: reinforces trust for sensitive journal data.

## Product Page Optimization

After the app is Ready for Distribution, test:

- Treatment A: search-led screenshots with "AI mood journal" messaging.
- Treatment B: emotional-payoff screenshots with "crowded thoughts" messaging.
- Treatment C: privacy-led screenshots if review/user trust becomes the biggest friction.

Success metric:
- Product page conversion rate first.
- Then downstream subscription trial start rate and 3-month subscription conversion.
