Target first submission: 5 screenshots for iPhone. Use real app screens, not marketing-only mockups.

- Onboarding result / "Ju Gets You" reveal

## Optional Screenshot 6 - Privacy and Control

Screen:

Purpose:

## Screenshot Rules

- Do not show fake medical claims.
- Do not show crisis advice as if Nuju replaces emergency support.
- Do not show unavailable subscription terms or a trial unless the App Store Connect introductory offer is active.
- Avoid "therapy", "diagnosis", "treatment", "cure", or similar claims in screenshot text.

- Screenshot 1 leads with "Feel understood in one minute"
- Screenshots emphasize emotional reveal, journaling, insights, AI companion, privacy
