# Entity and Distribution Checklist

## Tier 1: Owned-asset clarity

- keep category language consistent across landing, install, blog, and structured data
- make sure Nuju is consistently described as an AI journal, mood tracker, and emotional clarity app
- keep privacy claims identical everywhere they appear
- keep non-therapy framing visible on sensitive use-case pages

## Tier 2: Entity stack backlog

- official founder bio
- company LinkedIn page
- Product Hunt profile
- Crunchbase profile
- relevant app directories and software discovery profiles
- sameAs cleanup across site metadata and social profiles

## Tier 3: Category reinforcement

Prioritize mentions that reinforce one of these identities:

- AI journaling app
- mood tracker app
- journaling app for reflection

Good targets:

- app roundups
- alternatives pages
- productivity newsletters
- wellness creators
- therapist and coach resource pages
- journaling communities

## Promotion targets by page type

### Best X pages

- software directories
- app comparison publishers
- creator newsletters that share tool lists

### Alternative pages

- switch-intent roundups
- subreddit and community resource lists where brand comparisons already happen
- newsletters focused on productivity or self-improvement tools

### Use-case pages

- wellness blogs
- anxiety, ADHD, and self-awareness newsletters
- creator communities that discuss routines, reflection, and habit support

## Outreach rule

Each major recommendation page should have a short promotion list before publish:

1. 10 directories or roundup targets
2. 5 creator or newsletter targets
3. 5 community or expert targets

## Measurement

Track at minimum:

- impressions and clicks in Search Console
- page-level reveal starts
- page-level install visits
- ChatGPT referral traffic when available
- queries that begin sending recommendation or comparison intent

## Postpone for now

- deep backlink analysis without Ahrefs or Semrush
- NLP entity scoring without strong extraction data
- dashboard work before the recommendation pages are producing signal
